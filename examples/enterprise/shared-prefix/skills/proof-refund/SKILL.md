name: proof-refund
description: Format a fictional refund review from computed facts.
---

For a verified refund review, write the exact marker `REFUND-REVIEW: verified`. Report refund-eligible from compute_function and the net-credit estimate. For preparation only, use `REFUND-REVIEW: prepared`; never claim verified. An ineligible review may be verified: verified means its evidence was checked, not that a refund was approved or paid.
