# Acme returns and refunds policy

Version 4.2. Applies to all consumer orders placed through the Acme web store.

## Return window

- A customer may return an item **within 30 days of the delivery date**. The
  delivery date, not the order date, starts the clock.
- Past 30 days the order is out of window. Out-of-window returns are refused by
  default; only a manager may grant an exception, and only for a manufacturing
  defect.
- Items must be returned in their original packaging. Missing packaging costs a
  15% restocking fee.

## Refund method and timing

- Refunds go back to the original payment method. Store credit is offered only
  when the customer asks for it.
- The refund is issued once the returned parcel is scanned into the Leeds
  fulfilment centre, and settles within **5 business days**.
- Return shipping is paid by the customer, except for a damaged or incorrect
  item, where Acme pays and sends a prepaid label.

## Value thresholds

- Refunds **above 250 USD require manager approval** before they are issued. The
  agent may confirm eligibility but must not promise the money.
- Refunds at or below 250 USD are issued by the agent handling the case.

## Non-returnable items

- Opened consumables (filter papers, cleaning tablets, coffee).
- Gift cards and digital credit.
- Anything marked "final sale" at the time of purchase.

## Damaged on arrival

A customer reporting damage within 48 hours of delivery is sent a replacement
immediately, with no return required for items under 100 USD. Photographs are
requested but are not a condition of the replacement.
