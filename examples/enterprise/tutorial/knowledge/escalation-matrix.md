# Acme support escalation matrix

## Escalate to a manager

- Any refund above 250 USD, before it is issued.
- Any out-of-window return the customer disputes.
- A second complaint about the same order.
- Any mention of a chargeback, a regulator, or legal action.

Managers answer within one business day. Tell the customer that timeframe
explicitly rather than promising "soon".

## Escalate to the logistics desk

- A shipment lost in transit (14+ days) once the replacement has been sent.
- A carrier claim the carrier has refused.
- Repeated mis-delivery to the same address.

## Handle without escalating

- Refund eligibility questions inside the 30-day window and at or below 250 USD.
- Tracking and delivery-status questions.
- Replacements for damage reported within 48 hours of delivery, under 100 USD.

## What every reply must contain

1. The order id.
2. The rule that decided the outcome, in plain words.
3. The single next step, and who takes it.
